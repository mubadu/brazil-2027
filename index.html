The Amazon in late April

JUNGLE · 13 NIGHTS ONLY

High water: the river floods into the forest, so instead of long hikes you canoe through the trees, at canopy level.

Wildlife: flood season is the best time to see monkeys and sloths at eye level from the canoe. Pink river dolphins live here.

Rain + bugs: it's the wet season, so bring a rain jacket, a dry bag and repellent. Short jungle walks still run on higher ground.
